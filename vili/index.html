Vili Family

Vili Family is a private, single-household assistant run by Jaroslav Povolný on his own computer. It is not a public service and is not offered to other users.

With the account owner's consent it reads and manages the owner's Google Calendar and reads the owner's Google Photos, so that the household assistant can answer questions such as "what is on my calendar this week" and add events on request.
